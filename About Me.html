About Me

I'm a web developer based in Cape Town, South Africa. I've been building things for 1 year through the MSITA Web Development course now, mostly working with small web pages and startups.

What I love about this work is that every project is different. One week I'm building an online store, the next I'm working on a booking system or a content management tool. It keeps things interesting.

I didn't take the easy route into development. These days I spend most of my time with JavaScript and its various frameworks, but I'm comfortable working across the full stack. I like being able to take a project from concept to deployment without having to hand it off to someone else.

How I Work

I prefer to keep things simple and straightforward. Before writing any code, I spend time understanding what actually needs to be built and why. A lot of problems can be solved with simpler solutions than you'd think.

I'm big on communication. I'd rather over-communicate than leave clients or collaborators guessing. Regular updates, clear timelines, and being honest when something isn't working—that's how I like to operate.

Code quality matters to me. I write clean, documented code that other developers can understand. I also test things properly before calling them done. Rushing to finish just means fixing bugs later.

I'm always learning. This field changes fast, and what worked two years ago might not be the best approach today. I read documentation, follow other developers, and regularly try new tools to see what's worth adopting.
